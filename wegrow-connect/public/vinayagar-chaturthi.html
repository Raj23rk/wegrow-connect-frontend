FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate?

All collegiate students (Arts, Science, Engineering, Commerce, Diploma, MBA) from any college or institute in Tamil Nadu.

Are drawing sheets provided?

Yes, standard high-grade A3 drawing sheets will be given to each registered student at the venue.

What materials should I bring?

Bring your own drawing pencils, erasers, paints (watercolor/acrylic), pastels, sketch pens, and a drawing board.

Is there any registration fee?

No, registration is 100% free of cost.
